This page separates them for every part of MicroVerse, including the parts where the answer is "not validated".

Limits

What it does not establish.

    {% for caveat in TIER_CAVEATS %}
  • {{ caveat }}
    • {% endfor %}
  • Replication within one study's own samples is an easier test than replication in an independently collected cohort. These halves share protocol, population and sequencing run; a true external replication would not.
  • The experiment validates the ordering and the tiers' relative meaning. It does not license reading a tier as a probability for any particular taxon in your data.
