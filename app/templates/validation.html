The few differences sit at q = 0.05 and come from the input, not the statistics: the paper's relative abundances count reads that have no genus; a genus table cannot. One further cohort, with four controls, was refused by MicroVerse's five-per-group minimum. Three cohorts uploaded to this site gave results identical to the local runs.

The paper also publishes genera that move with disease across many studies. Reproducing all of it

Every number on this page is regenerable.

Nothing here is asserted from memory. Each check is a script that reports SKIPPED with an install hint when its dependency is missing, never a silent pass.
