Is your finding robust to the analysis you chose?

Why this exists

One analysis is a sample of size one.

01

The problem

Rarefy or not, which transformation, which test, which threshold: each choice is defensible and each can change the answer. A paper reports one set of choices and one p-value, and never shows what the other defensible sets would have said.

Tierney et al. fitted six million models across 15 cohorts: one taxon in three flipped the sign of its association, and 84% of published associations could be recovered as significant by picking the right model. PLOS Biology 2022;20(3):e3001556.

02

The approach

Run the whole space instead of one point in it. MicroVerse enumerates every combination of the contested choices, removes the combinations that are statistically incoherent, fits the rest, and reports the distribution of answers for every taxon, together with the choice that moves it.

Not a new statistical method: it orchestrates published methods inside published frameworks (multiverse analysis, specification curves, vibration of effects).

The unit of analysis

What is a specification?

One complete analysis: one value for every contested choice, applied in a fixed order, producing one p-value and one effect size per taxon. Change any single choice and you have a different {{ dfn('specification') }}.

MicroVerse counts every combination, rules out the incoherent ones (for example, rarefying and then applying TMM corrects library size twice), and runs all the rest. The seven choices, and why each is contested.

Reading the answer

Every taxon gets one of six labels.

Two numbers decide it: how often the taxon is significant across the analyses that tested it, and how often they agree on which group it is higher in. The three most telling:

ROBUST Robust to analytical choice

Significant in at least 80% of the analyses, in a consistent direction. The conclusion does not depend on which defensible pipeline you picked.

CONDITIONAL Depends on the analytical choice

Significant in some analyses and not others. Whether you would report it depends on a choice, and the results page shows which one.

UNSTABLE Direction not determined

The analyses do not agree on which group the taxon is higher in. There is no stable finding to report.

Limitations

{% call callout('red', 'What MicroVerse does not claim') %}
  • Robust is not true. A taxon robust to analytical choice is not thereby biologically real. Confounding, contamination and batch effects survive a multiverse intact.
  • Nothing here is causal. A difference that survives every analysis is still an association between two groups.
  • The analyses are not independent. They are re-analyses of the same samples, so “significant in 86%” is not 86 replications and not a probability.
  • The space is bounded. It covers what is defensible and computable, and every run reports what was enumerated, what was ruled out and why.

What your data must satisfy 4 rules
  • At least 10 samples, 5 per group. Below this nearly every specification is underpowered, so the spread of answers measures sampling noise rather than analytical choice.
  • At least 10 taxa, at most 1,500. Above 1,500, MicroVerse collapses to genus if a taxonomy allows it.
  • Exactly two groups. No multi-group, continuous or ordinal outcomes; no paired or longitudinal designs.
  • Counts or relative abundances, never negatives. A negative value means the table was already transformed, and transformation is one of the choices MicroVerse must apply itself.
