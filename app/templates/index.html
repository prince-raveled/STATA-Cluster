The problem

Every choice is defensible and each can change the answer, but a paper reports one.

1 in 3taxa flipped the sign of their association

The approach

Run the whole space, not one point: enumerate, rule out the incoherent, fit the rest, report the distribution and the choice that moves it.

Reading the answer

Every taxon gets one of six labels.

Decided by how often it is significant, and how often the analyses agree on its direction.

ROBUST Robust to analytical choice

Significant in ≥ 80% of analyses, one direction.

CONDITIONAL Depends on the analytical choice

UNSTABLE Direction not determined

Limitations

{% call callout('red', 'What MicroVerse does not claim') %}
  • Robust is not true. Confounding and batch effects survive every analysis.
  • Nothing here is causal. It is an association between two groups.
  • Not independent. “Significant in 86%” is not 86 replications.
  • The space is bounded. Every run reports what was ruled out and why.

What your data must satisfy 4 rules
  • At least 10 samples, 5 per group. Below this nearly every specification is underpowered, so the spread of answers measures sampling noise rather than analytical choice.
  • At least 10 taxa, at most 1,500. Above 1,500, MicroVerse collapses to genus if a taxonomy allows it.
  • Exactly two groups. No multi-group, continuous or ordinal outcomes; no paired or longitudinal designs.
  • Counts or relative abundances, never negatives. A negative value means the table was already transformed, and transformation is one of the choices MicroVerse must apply itself.
