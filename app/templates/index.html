The damage, measured

This is not a hypothetical problem.

Tierney et al. fitted six million models across fifteen cohorts to ask how much of a published microbiome association is the data and how much is the analyst. The answer was not reassuring.

What has to be true

Four hard constraints, each with a reason rather than a rule.

  1. 01
    At least 10 samples, 5 per group Below this the multiverse is noise: nearly every specification is underpowered, so the spread of answers measures sampling variability rather than analytical choice.
  2. 02
    At least 10 taxa, at most 1,500 Multiple-testing behaviour is half of what the multiverse measures, and it is meaningless below ten. Above 1,500, MicroVerse collapses to genus if a taxonomy allows it.
  3. 03
    Exactly two group levels v1 compares two groups. No multi-group, continuous or ordinal outcomes; no paired or longitudinal designs.
  4. 04
    Counts or relative abundances, never negatives A negative value means the table has already been transformed — and the transformation is fork 3, which MicroVerse has to apply itself.

Three modes, never one crossed grid

Fully crossing all seven forks is over 500,000 specifications, which no web request can run. So the modes are separated, and you choose after your data is validated.

~1 sQuick — forks 1–4, four elementary methods, FDR
~19 sFull — plus ANCOM-BC, ALDEx2, PyDESeq2
~5 sCovariate — every subset of your covariates

Model fitting on the demo cohorts, measured on four cores. A complete run also apportions the variation between the choices and saves the results, which on this site takes one to two minutes. The specification budget is 90 s for Quick and 10 minutes for Full.

Stated plainly

What MicroVerse does not claim.

Not a new statistical method. It orchestrates published methods inside a published framework — multiverse analysis, specification curves, vibration of effects.

Not truth. A taxon robust to analytical choice is not thereby biologically real. Confounding, contamination and batch effects survive a multiverse intact.

Not a licence to cherry-pick. There is no “best specification” export on the page, in the API, or in the download bundle. Exports always contain the full distribution.

Not exhaustive. The grid is bounded by what is defensible and computable, and every run reports how many specifications it enumerated, how many it pruned, and why.
