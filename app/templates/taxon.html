What this page cannot tell you.

  • The analyses are not independent evidence. They are {{ row.n_specs_tested|commafy }} re-analyses of the same samples, so "significant in {{ row.frac_significant|percent(0) }}" describes how much the conclusion depends on your choices — it is not a p-value, not a probability, and not {{ row.n_specs_tested|commafy }} replications.
  • Statistical stability is not biological importance. A taxon can be stable and trivial, or unstable and real.
  • Nothing here is causal. A difference between groups that survives every analytical choice is still an association.
  • The effect size shown above comes from the abundance table, not from a model, so it is identical across every statistical test and does not change when an analysis adjusts for a covariate (its spread across 32 adjustment sets is exactly zero). Adjustment does matter here — it shows up in whether the taxon is called significant, not in this number. Do not quote it as a regression coefficient.
